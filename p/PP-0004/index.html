English

GEYA GYM9 two-pole (2P) miniature circuit breaker (MCB), designed to protect electrical circuits against overload and short circuit. Its compact design suits installation in distribution boards on a DIN rail.

Technical specifications

  • Brand: GEYA
  • Model: GYM9
  • Type: MCB – Miniature Circuit Breaker
  • Poles: 2P
  • Available rated current: 2A to 125A
  • Rated voltage: 400V AC
  • Frequency: 50/60Hz
  • Breaking capacity: 6kA (6000A) (for the pictured breaker)
  • Tripping curve: Type C
  • Standard marked: IEC/EN 60898-1
  • Mounting: DIN rail

Applications: protection of electrical circuits in homes, shops and distribution boards, depending on the load and protection requirements.
