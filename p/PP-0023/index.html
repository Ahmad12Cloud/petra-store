English

Single-pole (1P) fuse holder for 10×38 cylindrical fuses, available for AC and DC circuits, used to protect solar PV strings and AC / DC circuits against short circuit and overcurrent, with a compact design for installation in distribution boards on a DIN rail.

Available in three colors

  • White: AC fuse holder – model AC-PV0 – up to 690V AC.
  • Red: DC fuse holder – model DC-PV0 – up to 1000V DC.
  • Black: DC fuse holder – model DC-PV0 – up to 1000V DC.

Technical specifications

  • Model: AC-PV0 / DC-PV0
  • Type: AC / DC Fuse Holder
  • Poles: 1P
  • Fuse size: 10×38 mm
  • Rated current: up to 32A
  • Rated voltage: 690V AC (white) – 1000V DC (red and black)
  • Colors: white / red / black
  • Mounting: DIN rail

Applications: the white (AC) holder protects AC lines such as inverter outputs and distribution boards; the red and black (DC) holders protect solar PV strings, combiner boxes and DC circuits in solar energy systems, depending on the fuse rating and protection requirements.
