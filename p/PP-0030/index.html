English

GEYA GPS8-03 3-Phase Voltage Protector: protects equipment and loads against over voltage, under voltage and overload (over current). It disconnects the power automatically on any fault and reconnects automatically when the voltage returns to normal. The display shows the voltage and current of each phase (L1 – L2 – L3).

Available in two ratings (choose the current when ordering):

  • 63A
  • 80A

Protections

  • Over voltage: adjustable 230V – 300V – factory setting 275V
  • Under voltage: adjustable 145V – 210V – factory setting 175V
  • Overload (over current): adjustable 1A up to 63A or 80A depending on the model
  • Phase asymmetry: adjustable or OFF
  • Phase sequence: ON / OFF

Technical specifications

  • Brand: GEYA
  • Model: GPS8-03
  • Type: 3-phase voltage protector
  • Supply voltage: 220V AC (L1, L2, L3 – N)
  • Operating range: 80V – 400V
  • Frequency: 45 – 65Hz
  • Rated current: 63A / 80A (AC1)
  • Voltage trip delay: 0.1 – 10 s
  • Reset time: 2 – 900 s – default 30 s
  • Measurement: True RMS
  • Display: digital LED, voltage and current per phase
  • Mounting: 35mm DIN rail
