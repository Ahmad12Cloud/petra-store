English

OPEN OPWG3 Tuya Wi-Fi smart voltage and current protector (VA Protect + kW) for single-phase 230V AC circuits (1P+N). It disconnects the supply automatically on over-voltage, under-voltage or overcurrent above the set limit, and reconnects automatically when the supply returns to normal (auto reconnect). A digital display shows voltage (V), current (A) and power (kW) in real time, it meters energy consumption (kWh), and the device can be monitored and controlled remotely over Wi-Fi with the Tuya / Smart Life app, with a built-in timer. DIN rail mounting.

VA Protect + kW (Volt + Amp + Kilowatt)

  • V: over / under voltage protection with live voltage reading.
  • A: overcurrent protection at the set limit (1A – 63A) with live current reading.
  • kW: live power reading of the connected load.

Technical specifications

  • Brand: OPEN
  • Model: OPWG3
  • Poles: 1P+N
  • Rated voltage: 230V AC
  • Overcurrent protection: programmable 1A – 63A
  • Power display: 0.0 – 63.0 kW
  • Voltage protection: over / under voltage
  • Auto reconnect: Yes
  • Timer: Yes
  • Remote control: Wi-Fi – Tuya / Smart Life app
  • Display: digital V / A / kW
  • Mounting: DIN rail

Installation: supply in at the top (IN), load out at the bottom (OUT); a suitable MCB upstream is recommended.

Applications: protection of homes, shops and electrical appliances against voltage fluctuations, over and under voltage, with energy monitoring and remote control, whether the supply is grid, generator or inverter.
