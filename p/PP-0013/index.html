English

GEYA GWS10-WJ Tuya Wi-Fi smart circuit breaker for single-phase 230V AC, 50/60Hz circuits (1P+N), made in China. It is controlled remotely over Wi-Fi with the Tuya / Smart Life app and disconnects automatically on over-voltage, under-voltage, overcurrent above the set limit, over-power or over-temperature, then reconnects automatically when the supply returns to normal (auto reconnect). It meters energy, shows voltage, current and power in the app, and records consumption by hour, day, month and year. Slim one-module design (18mm) with manual ON / OFF button and status light. DIN rail mounting.

Features

  • Energy metering (kWh): live voltage, current and power, with consumption history.
  • Timer / schedule: scheduled ON / OFF, countdown, cycle and sunrise / sunset timing.
  • Remote control: from anywhere over the internet; works with Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant voice control.
