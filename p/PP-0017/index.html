English

GEYA GYHC-63 modular contactor, 2-pole (2P), available in 63A and 100A, with one normally open (NO) contact – terminals 1-2, and one normally closed (NC) contact – terminals R1-R2. It switches the load automatically when the coil (A1 – A2) is energized, e.g. by a timer, twilight switch or sensor: the NO contact closes and the NC contact opens. Suitable for lighting, water heaters, heating and household / commercial loads, and for changeover between two lines. DIN rail mounting.

Technical specifications

  • Brand: GEYA
  • Model: GYHC-63

  • Contacts: 1NO + 1NC (1-2 NO, R1-R2 NC)
  • Available ratings: 63A (model GYHC-63) and 100A (model GYHC-100)
  • Rated operational current (Ie), 63A model: 63A (AC-7a) / 20A (AC-7b)
  • Rated operational current (Ie), 100A model: 100A (AC-7a)
  • Rated operational voltage (Ue): 250V
  • Rated insulation voltage (Ui): 500V
  • Coil voltage: 230V AC – 50/60Hz
  • Standard: IEC 61095 / EN 61095 – CE
  • Mounting: DIN rail 35mm

Note: AC-7a for resistive loads (lighting, heaters), AC-7b for small household motors.
