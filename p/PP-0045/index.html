English

ICHYTI LW4R four-pole (4P) automatic transfer switch (ATS), industrial size for large loads, that automatically transfers the load between two sources (grid and generator): when the main source fails it switches to the standby source, and when the main source returns it switches back. It works automatically (Auto) with a transfer motor, or manually (Manual) with the operating handle.

Available ratings (choose when ordering): 100A – 160A – 200A – 250A – 315A.

Technical specifications

  • Brand: ICHYTI
  • Model: LW4R
  • Available rated current: 100A – 160A – 200A – 250A – 315A
  • Poles: 4P (3 phases + neutral – A B C N)
  • Rated voltage (Ue): 400V AC – 50/60Hz
  • Transfer motor control voltage: 220V AC
  • Class: PC – transfer switch only, no overload or short-circuit protection, so a suitable protective breaker must be installed before it
  • Positions: Normal (I) – Reserve (II)
  • Operation: automatic / manual (Auto / Manual)
  • Mounting: screw mounting inside the panel, with copper terminals
  • Standard: IEC EN 60947-6-1 / GB/T 14048.11

Applications: automatic transfer between grid and generator for buildings, shops, factories and industrial loads, inside main distribution boards.
