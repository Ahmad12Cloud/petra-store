English

GEYA GRV8-10 3-Phase Voltage Monitoring Relay for 3-phase + neutral lines. It protects motors and loads against over voltage, under voltage, phase loss, wrong phase sequence and phase asymmetry. On a fault the output relay drops after the set delay, and it reconnects after the reset time once the fault clears.

Protections

  • Over voltage (U>): 2% – 20% above nominal
  • Under voltage (U<): 2% – 20% below nominal
  • Phase asymmetry (Asy): 5% – 15%
  • Phase loss: L1 / L2 / L3
  • Phase sequence: ABC / ACB

Technical specifications

  • Brand: GEYA
  • Model: GRV8-10
  • Type: 3-phase voltage monitoring relay – 3 phase + N
  • Nominal voltage (Un): selectable 127 – 265V (phase – neutral)
  • Trip delay (Tt): 0.1 – 10 s
  • Reset time (Rst.t): 0.1 – 10 s
  • Output: 2 × SPDT – terminals 11-12-14 and 21-22-24
  • Contact rating: 8A / AC1 – 250V AC
  • Indicators: Un (green) – U> – U</Asy – R
  • Mounting: 35mm DIN rail
