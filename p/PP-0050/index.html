English

Aoasis AGK-04N high & low voltage control relay for 3 phase + neutral 380V networks. It monitors the phase voltages, and if the voltage goes above the high limit or below the low limit you set, it opens the contact after the delay time, protecting motors and loads.

According to the manufacturer: designed to protect systems from voltage changes, and to protect industrial 3 phase motors from overheating and damage.

Setting

  • U> knob (top): high voltage limit from 400 to 460V
  • U< knob (lower left): low voltage limit from 260 to 360V
  • t knob (lower right): delay before tripping from 0.1 to 10 seconds, so it does not trip on short voltage dips or spikes

Indicators (LED)

  • RLY: relay energised
  • U>: high voltage error
  • U<: low voltage error
  • L12 – L23 – L31: phase voltages

Technical specifications

  • Brand: Aoasis
  • Model: AGK-04N
  • Type: High & low voltage control relay
  • Operating voltage (Un): 3×380V AC + neutral – 50/60Hz
  • High voltage limit (U>): 400 – 460V adjustable
  • Low voltage limit (U<): 260 – 360V adjustable
  • Delay time (t): 0.1 – 10 s
  • Relay output: 1 changeover contact (1 – 2 – 3) – 5A 250V AC
  • Operating temperature: -20°C to +55°C – humidity up to 85%
  • Protection: IP20
  • Certifications: CE
  • Mounting: DIN rail 35mm

Terminals

  • N – L1 – L2 – L3: supply and monitoring
  • 1 – 2 – 3: relay output (2 common)

Applications: protection of 3 phase motors, pumps and compressors, control panels and generators against high and low voltage.
