English

ICHYTI AVP-150 auto-reclosing power protector for single-phase lines (phase + neutral). It protects appliances and the line against overvoltage, undervoltage, overcurrent, earth leakage and over-temperature: on any fault it disconnects both phase and neutral, and when the fault clears it restores power automatically after the set recovery delay. It shows voltage and current on its display, with an audible alarm and a fault log.

Available in two ratings (choose when ordering)

  • 100A
  • 150A

Protections

  • Overvoltage (P-3): adjustable 120V – 300V or OFF – default 270V
  • Undervoltage (P-4): adjustable 80V – 210V or OFF – default 170V
  • Overcurrent (P-7): adjustable from 1A up to 100A or 150A depending on model, or OFF
  • Residual current (P-11): 10 – 300mA or OFF – default 30mA – trips within 100ms
  • Over-temperature (P-12): 50°C – 120°C or OFF – default 85°C
  • Surge protection: Class III

Technical specifications

  • Brand: ICHYTI
  • Model: AVP-150
  • Type: Auto-reclosing power protector
  • Poles: 1P+N – cuts both N & L
  • Rated voltage (Ue): AC 230V – 50/60Hz
  • Operating range: AC 80 – 300V
  • Available rated current (Ie): 100A / 150A
  • Power-on delay (P-1): 1 – 999 s – default 5 s
  • Recovery delay (P-2): 1 – 999 s – default 30 s
  • Accuracy: Class 0.5
  • Insulation voltage (Ui): 450V
  • Protection class: IP20
  • Display: digital voltage and current, with >U<, >I<, IΔn and POWER indicators
  • Extras: audible alarm – fault log up to 999 – password lock – leakage test button (T)
  • Dimensions: 50 × 111 × 70 mm
  • Mounting: DIN rail 35mm

Fault codes on display

  • PD: power failure
  • UU: undervoltage
  • OU: overvoltage
  • OC: overcurrent
  • LE: leakage
  • HT: over-temperature

Note: install a suitable circuit breaker before it; installation must be done by a qualified electrician.

Applications: protecting homes, shops, air conditioners and appliances from power fluctuations and leakage, especially with generators and subscription power.
