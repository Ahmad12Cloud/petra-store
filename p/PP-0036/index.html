CHYT YTTM1-250 two-pole (2P) DC molded case circuit breaker (MCCB), supplied ready-mounted inside a waterproof and dustproof plastic enclosure with a transparent opening cover, designed to protect DC circuits in solar energy and battery systems against overload and short circuit, with a Push to Trip button to test the tripping mechanism, and suitable for outdoor installation.
