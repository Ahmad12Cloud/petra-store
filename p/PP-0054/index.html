حسب الشركة المصنّعة: مصمّم لمراقبة تيار الحمل المتناوب (AC) وفولتاج الفاز تبعه بشكل متواصل.

English

Aoasis ADAV DIN rail ammeter and voltmeter for single phase lines. It shows the current on a red display and the voltage on a yellow display at the same time, and comes with a ring current transformer (CT) that the cable passes through, so the load line does not need to be cut.

According to the manufacturer: designed to monitor both the AC current drawn by the loads and the voltage of the relevant phase continuously.

Available sizes

  • ADAV-60: current 1A to 63A – with CT60
  • ADAV-100: current 10A to 100A – with CT120
  • ADAV-250: current 10A to 250A – with CT300

Readings

  • Current: in A – red display
  • Voltage: in V – yellow display
  • True RMS: accurate readings even with distorted waveforms (inverters, motors, electronic loads)

Technical specifications

  • Brand: Aoasis
  • Model: ADAV-60 / ADAV-100 / ADAV-250
  • Type: DIN rail ammeter and voltmeter – single phase
  • Operating voltage (Un): 150V – 270V AC
  • Frequency: 50/60Hz
  • Operating power: <6VA
  • Voltage measurement range: 10V – 500V AC
  • Current measurement range: 1–63A / 10–100A / 10–250A depending on size
  • Current transformer: CT60 / CT120 / CT300 depending on size
  • Measurement precision: ±1%
  • Display: 2 × 3 digits – 9mm
  • Connection: screw terminals – 1.5mm² cable
  • Operating temperature: -20°C to +55°C
  • Protection class: IP41
  • Operating altitude: below 2000 m
  • Weight: <200g / <240g / <250g depending on size
  • Mounting: DIN rail or panel

Terminals

  • N – L: device supply and voltage measurement
  • k – l: current transformer (CT) terminals

Applications: monitoring loads in homes and shops, generator and subscription panels, solar and inverter panels, and the current draw of air conditioners and pumps.
