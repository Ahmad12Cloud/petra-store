English

GEYA THC-109 light control (twilight) switch: it switches the lighting on automatically at dusk and off at daylight using a photocell sensor on a cable. A trimmer sets the switching light level (5 – 150 LUX), and a selector gives three modes: OFF / AUTO / ON. Suitable for street, garden, facade and outdoor lighting. DIN rail mounting.
