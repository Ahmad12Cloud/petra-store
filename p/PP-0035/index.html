English

Clip-on cable markers (numbers 0–9) that snap onto the wire to number and identify conductors inside distribution and control panels, making wiring, testing and maintenance easier and preventing connection mistakes.

Technical specifications

  • Type: Clip-on cable markers – numbers 0–9
  • Numbers: 0 to 9
  • Color: color coded – a different color for each number: 0 black, 1 brown, 2 red, 3 orange, 4 yellow, 5 green, 6 blue, 7 violet, 8 grey, 9 white
  • Available sizes (Cross section): 1.5 / 2.5 / 4 / 6 mm²
  • Installation: snap on by hand, no tools needed

Applications: numbering wires in distribution and control panels, breakers, contactors, relays and solar PV systems.
