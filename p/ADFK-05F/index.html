English

Aoasis ADFK-05F Digital Phase Failure Relay, designed to protect 3-phase motors and loads from mains faults. It continuously monitors the three phases; on a fault it switches its output relay (1 – 2 – 3) to drop the contactor and protect the load, and after the fault clears it restores automatically after the set delay.

Protections

  • Phase loss: L1 / L2 / L3
  • Phase sequence: ABC / ACB
  • Phase asymmetry: adjustable 5% – 25%
  • Voltage error: Volt Err LED indication

Display: 3 LED displays showing the line-to-line voltages (L1-L2 / L2-L3 / L3-L1), with Asm Err – Volt Err – Out indicators.

Technical specifications

  • Brand: Aoasis
  • Model: ADFK-05F
  • Type: digital 3-phase phase failure relay
  • Rated voltage (Un): 3 × 380V AC – 50/60Hz
  • Asymmetry: adjustable 5% – 25%
  • Waiting time (t): adjustable 0.1 – 10 s
  • Output contact: 1 – 2 – 3 (NO / NC) – 5A 250V AC (resistive load)
  • Connection: L1 – L2 – L3, no neutral
  • Mounting: 35mm DIN rail
