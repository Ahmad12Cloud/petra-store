English

GEYA GYPV-1038 single-pole (1P) DC fuse holder for 10×38 cylindrical fuses, used to protect solar PV strings and DC circuits against short circuit and overcurrent, with a compact design for installation in distribution boards on a DIN rail.

Available in two versions

  • Without LED indicator: standard version.
  • With LED indicator: lights up when the fuse blows, so a blown fuse is identified quickly without testing.

Technical specifications

  • Rated current: 32A
  • Rated voltage: 1000V DC
  • LED indicator: available with or without
  • Mounting: DIN rail

Applications: protection of solar PV strings, combiner boxes and DC circuits in solar energy systems, depending on the fuse rating and protection requirements.
