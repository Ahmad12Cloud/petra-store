English

GEYA GYJ1-22 contactor relay with a 220V AC coil. It works like a contactor for small circuits: when the coil (A1 – A2) is energised it switches 4 contacts at the same time, the 2 open contacts close and the 2 closed contacts open. Used for control and signalling circuits and for linking contactors.

Contacts

  • 2NO (normally open): 13 – 14 and 43 – 44
  • 2NC (normally closed): 21 – 22 and 31 – 32
  • Coil: A1 – A2
