English

GEYA GWS9-DW Tuya Wi-Fi smart circuit breaker for single-phase 230V AC circuits (1P+N), made in China. It is controlled remotely over Wi-Fi with the Tuya / Smart Life app and disconnects automatically on over-voltage, under-voltage, overcurrent above the programmed limit or over-temperature, then reconnects automatically when the supply returns to normal (auto reconnect). The LCD display shows switch status (ON / OFF), total energy (kWh), time and date, with navigation buttons. DIN rail mounting.

Features

  • LCD display: status, energy and time shown on the breaker.
  • Energy metering (kWh): consumption on the display and in the app.
  • Timer / schedule: ON / OFF at set times.
  • Remote control: ON / OFF from anywhere over the internet.

Technical specifications

  • Brand: GEYA
  • Model: GWS9-DW
  • Type: Tuya Wi-Fi Smart Circuit Breaker – LCD Display
  • Poles: 1P+N
  • Rated voltage: 230V AC
  • Overcurrent protection: programmable 1A – 63A
  • Voltage protection: over / under voltage
  • Over-temperature protection: Yes
  • Energy metering: kWh
  • Auto reconnect: Yes
  • Timer / schedule: Yes
  • Remote control: Wi-Fi 2.4GHz – Tuya / Smart Life app
  • Display: LCD
  • Mounting: DIN rail

Installation: supply on Power terminals (1 and N), load on Load terminal (2); a suitable MCB upstream is recommended.

Applications: protection of homes, shops and electrical appliances against voltage fluctuations, over and under voltage, with energy monitoring, remote control and scheduling, whether the supply is grid, generator or inverter.
