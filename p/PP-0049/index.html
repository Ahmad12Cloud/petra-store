English

Aoasis AERV-08 On Delay time relay, covering 0.1 seconds up to 100 hours across 6 ranges.

Operation (On Delay): when supply is applied, the timer counts the set time; when it ends the output contact changes over and stays changed over as long as supply is present. When supply is removed it returns to its normal state.

Setting

  • Lower knob (Time Steps): selects the range – A: 10s – B: 100s – C: 10m – D: 100m – E: 10h – F: 100h
  • Upper knob (t): sets the time from 0.1 to 10 of the selected range. Example: range C (10m) with the knob on 5 = 5 minutes
  • ON LED: supply present – OUT LED: output switched (time elapsed)

Technical specifications

  • Brand: Aoasis
  • Model: AERV-08
  • Type: On delay timer
  • Time range: 0.1 s to 100 h (6 ranges A – F)
  • Supply voltage: 220V AC – 50/60Hz on A1-A2, or 24V AC/DC on A2-A3
  • Operating range: 150 – 260V AC / 24V DC ±10%
  • Relay output: 1 changeover contact (1 – 2 – 3) – 5A 250V AC (resistive load)
  • Power consumption: < 4VA
  • Indicators (LED): ON and OUT
  • Operating temperature: -20°C to +55°C – humidity up to 85%
  • Protection: IP20
  • Certifications: CE
  • Mounting: DIN rail 35mm or screws

Terminals

  • A1 – A2: 220V AC supply
  • A2 – A3: 24V AC/DC supply
  • 1 – 2 – 3: relay output (2 common)

Applications: delaying the start of air conditioners, compressors, motors and pumps after power returns, and control and automation panels.
